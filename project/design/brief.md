## Signature element

<!-- The highest-value section in this document, when the product has a
real candidate for one. A signature element is:
  1. Recurring — appears in more than one place across the app.
  2. Functional, not decorative — does real work (shows real data,
     triggers a real action), so its repetition is earned rather than
     purely branding.
  3. Distinctive — a screenshot of just this element alone would be
     recognizable as belonging to this product specifically.

Not every product has an obvious candidate, and forcing one where none
exists produces arbitrary decoration rather than genuine identity. If a
real one exists, describe its behavior precisely enough that it can be
rendered consistently everywhere it appears — including whether a second,
deliberately distinct element might be needed later for a conceptually
different piece of data, rather than a recolored version of the first
one meaning something structurally different. -->
